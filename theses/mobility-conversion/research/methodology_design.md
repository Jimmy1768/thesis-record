## Required Separation

Outcome dimensions measure transport quality and usable access.

Explanatory axes may help explain variation in outcomes.

The design must not mix the two by treating wealth, demand pressure, urban form,
governance, or growth stage as direct score components.

## External Research Handling

Deep Research and other outsourced research returns may help discover sources,
terminology, measurement options, and counterarguments. They are not accepted as
evidence until independently verified and converted into ThesisRecord source
notes or methodology records.
