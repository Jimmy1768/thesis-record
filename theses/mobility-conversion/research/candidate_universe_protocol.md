# Candidate Universe Protocol

Status: candidate scaffold. No MTR is selected yet.

## Purpose

The candidate universe protocol exists to prevent selection by reputation,
fame, intuition, or convenience.

## Prohibited Selection Bases

Do not select an MTR because it is:

- famous for transit;
- famous for congestion;
- famous for walkability;
- highly ranked for livability;
- commonly described as a good city;
- commonly described as a bad city;
- located in a rich country;
- located in a poor country;
- personally familiar or interesting.

## Required Before Selection

Before naming candidate MTRs, define:

- boundary rule;
- minimum source requirements;
- region-size inclusion rules;
- mode-scope rules;
- temporal window;
- geographic stratification, if any;
- exclusion rules;
- how missing data is handled.

The formation-stage boundary source hierarchy is defined in:

- `theses/mobility-conversion/research/boundary_source_decision_record_2026-06-28.md`

No MTR may be named until it passes the boundary admission gate in that decision
