# Source Note: Eurostat Household Budget Surveys As Affordability Input

Date inspected: 2026-06-30

Verification status: directly inspected official Eurostat Household Budget
Surveys page.

Sources:

- Eurostat, "Household Budget Surveys"
- URL: `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/household-budget-surveys`

## Source Authority

Eurostat household budget survey source family.

## What The Source Defines

Eurostat Household Budget Surveys describe household consumption expenditure
patterns across participating European statistical systems.

For Mobility Conversion, the relevant source-family function is household
transport expenditure context, especially where expenditure categories can be
read through transport-related consumption classifications.

## Outcome Dimension Fit

Primary fit:

- affordability.

Secondary fit:

- access to opportunities, as a cost-burden context if linked to income and
  access models.

## Usefulness For Mobility Conversion

Eurostat HBS is useful for:

- household transport expenditure context in European settings;
- comparing expenditure categories under a statistical framework;
- identifying whether transport cost burden can be measured beyond public
  transport fares;
- cross-checking affordability against income and living-condition sources.

## Limitations

- HBS geographies may not align with MTR boundaries.
- Survey waves and country coverage require vintage-specific review.
- Published tables may not provide enough local or metropolitan detail.
- Expenditure reflects realized travel and household constraints; it can miss
  foregone trips and suppressed demand.
- Transport spending must be interpreted with car dependency, service
  availability, income distribution, and household composition.
- The source does not itself provide public transport fare-policy detail.

## Suitability Judgment

Suitability: `indicator_source_family`.

Candidate outcome dimensions:

- `affordability`.

Not suitable as:

- standalone affordability metric;
- MTR denominator;
- direct proof of access quality;
- replacement for fare, income, or car-cost data.

## Methodology Implication

Eurostat HBS can support European affordability analysis, but only with explicit
geography, vintage, expenditure-category, and income-linkage caveats. It should
not be read as a direct MTR affordability outcome unless boundary alignment is
documented.
