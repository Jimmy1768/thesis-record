# Mobility Conversion

Status: candidate formation thesis.

Mobility Conversion studies why some Metropolitan Transport Regions convert
capacity, pressure, shape, governance, and growth conditions into usable access
better than others.

This is Thesis 2 in ThesisRecord. It is not yet a paper, ranking, scorecard, or
finished methodology.
