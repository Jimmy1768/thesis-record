# Framing Contract

Status: candidate source-truth scaffold. Not evidence, scoring, or paper prose.

## Core Question

Why do some Metropolitan Transport Regions convert resources, constraints,
institutional arrangements, and growth conditions into usable access better than
others?

## Approved Unit Language

Use:

- Metropolitan Transport Region
- MTR

Do not use:

- Metropolitan Transit Region

## Approved Outcome Frame

The outcome is usable access performance: how well the region's transport
system lets people reach opportunities and essential activities with acceptable
time, reliability, safety, affordability, physical accessibility, and resilience.

Candidate outcome dimensions:

- access to opportunities;
- network coverage and service availability;
- travel time and reliability;
- transportation safety;
- affordability;
- walking, cycling, and universal access;
- asset condition and resilience.

## Candidate Explanatory Axes

These are explanatory axes, not score components:

- economic capacity;
- demand intensity;
- urban form;
- governance fit;
- growth / maturity.

## External Query-Test Rule

Prompt-only outputs, query-test outputs, and ChatGPT Deep Research returns are
smoke tests and source leads. They are not evidence, source truth, accepted
methodology, accepted MTR boundaries, accepted candidate selection, or accepted
scores.

Each useful external research output must be converted into one of:

- an open question;
- a source lead;
- a source note after independent verification;
- a methodology candidate;
- rejected material.
