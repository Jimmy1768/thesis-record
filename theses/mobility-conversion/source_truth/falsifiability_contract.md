# Falsifiability Contract

Status: candidate scaffold. No prediction is accepted yet.

Mobility Conversion must produce claims that can be contradicted by evidence.

## Formation Rule

No final score, ranking, or comparative conclusion is accepted during candidate
formation.

## Candidate Failure Pressures

The thesis weakens if:

- usable access cannot be operationalized separately from reputation or
  livability;
- MTR boundaries cannot be defined consistently enough for comparison;
- data availability determines apparent performance more than real access;
- outcome dimensions collapse into incompatible measures that cannot be
  interpreted together;
- explanatory axes cannot be separated from outcomes;
- governance fit cannot be coded without subjective narrative dominance;
- growth / maturity is too confounded with wealth, age, or infrastructure
  inheritance to be useful;
- candidate selection cannot avoid reputation, fame, or intuition bias.

## Required Future Prediction Fields

Any future prediction or comparative claim should specify:

- claim;
- unit of analysis;
- MTR boundary rule;
- outcome dimension;
- measurement unit;
- expected direction;
- comparison group;
- timeframe;
- source or source class;
- confounders;
- failure condition.
