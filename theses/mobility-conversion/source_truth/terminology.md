# Terminology

Status: candidate terminology. Definitions may be revised during formation.

## Metropolitan Transport Region

Working unit of analysis for a metropolitan-scale transport system. The MTR
should capture the functional region where people travel across municipal
boundaries for work, education, services, care, commerce, and social life.

The exact boundary rule is not yet approved.

## Mobility Conversion

Working label for the region-level process by which economic capacity, demand
pressure, physical form, governance arrangements, and growth stage are converted
into usable access.

## Usable Access

Working label for transport quality as experienced through reachable
opportunities, service availability, travel time, reliability, safety,
affordability, walkability, bikeability, universal access, asset condition, and
resilience.

## Explanatory Axis

A candidate factor that may help explain differences in usable access
performance. An explanatory axis is not an outcome dimension and is not a score
component.

## Outcome Dimension

A candidate dimension of usable access performance. Outcome dimensions must be
defined before measurement and must remain separate from explanatory axes.
